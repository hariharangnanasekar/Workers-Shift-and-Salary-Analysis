# Workers Shift and Salary Analysis

This work aims to analyze the attendance and salary data of farm employees working in morning and evening shifts. The goal is to derive insights into employee work patterns, shift preferences, salary distribution, and workforce optimization to improve farm operations.

## Objective

The primary goal of this project is to analyze the attendance and salary data of farm workers to provide actionable insights. This analysis covers:

- Employee attendance patterns.

- Shift-specific salary distribution.

- Top earners and frequent workers.

- Optimization opportunities for cost and workforce management.

## Results

The project successfully provides:

- A detailed breakdown of total salaries paid.

- Insights into employee attendance and shift patterns.

- Identification of top-earning employees and their contributions.

- Visualizations to better understand workforce trends.

## Acknowledgment

This mini-project was created as an assessment by my father to analyze the workers' shifts and salaries in the farm field. The dataset consists of one month's data collected manually and compiled into an Excel sheet for analysis.
