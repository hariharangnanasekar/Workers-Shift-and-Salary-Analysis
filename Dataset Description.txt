## Dataset Description

The dataset consists of the following columns:

1)Date: The date of work.

2)Day: The day of the week.

3)Morning Shift: The name of the employee who worked in the morning shift.

4)Evening Shift: The name of the employee who worked in the evening shift.

5)Morning Shift Salary: The salary earned by the morning shift employee.

6)Evening Shift Salary: The salary earned by the evening shift employee.
